🔍 PROBLEM: "No speech detected"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This means your microphone is working, but it's not picking up your voice
loud enough. This is usually a VOLUME issue, not a permission issue.


✅ SOLUTION: USE THE MICROPHONE TEST TOOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

I've added a Microphone Test tool to your app!

Location: Right above the "Ask a Question" box

How to use:
1. Click "▶️ Test Microphone"
2. Speak into your microphone
3. Watch the level bar
4. You should see it move when you speak

What to look for:
✅ Level > 10 = Good! Microphone is working
⚠️ Level < 10 = Too quiet! Need to fix


🔧 FIX #1: INCREASE MICROPHONE VOLUME (WINDOWS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Open Sound Settings
- Right-click speaker icon in taskbar
- Click "Sound settings"
- Or search "Sound settings" in Windows

Step 2: Go to Input Settings
- Scroll down to "Input"
- Click on your microphone device

Step 3: Increase Volume
- Move "Input volume" slider to 100%
- Click "Test your microphone"
- Speak and watch the blue bar

Step 4: Advanced Settings
- Click "Device properties"
- Go to "Additional device properties"
- Click "Levels" tab
- Set Microphone to 100
- Set Microphone Boost to +20dB or +30dB
- Click OK


🔧 FIX #2: SELECT CORRECT MICROPHONE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You might have multiple microphones:
- Built-in laptop microphone
- External USB microphone
- Headset microphone
- Webcam microphone

How to check:
1. Use the Microphone Test tool
2. Select different microphones from dropdown
3. Test each one
4. Use the one that shows highest level


🔧 FIX #3: BROWSER SETTINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Chrome/Edge:
1. Go to chrome://settings/content/microphone
2. Check which microphone is selected
3. Make sure it's not blocked
4. Try selecting a different microphone

Firefox:
1. Go to about:preferences#privacy
2. Scroll to Permissions → Microphone
3. Click Settings
4. Check permissions


🔧 FIX #4: PHYSICAL CHECKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ Is microphone muted? (Check physical mute button)
✅ Is microphone plugged in properly?
✅ Is microphone close enough to your mouth?
✅ Is there background noise interfering?
✅ Try a different USB port (for external mics)


🔧 FIX #5: SPEAK LOUDER & CLOSER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Simple fixes:
✅ Speak LOUDER
✅ Move CLOSER to microphone (6-12 inches)
✅ Speak DIRECTLY into microphone
✅ Reduce background noise
✅ Close windows/doors

🔍 COMMON ISSUES & SOLUTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Issue: "No speech detected"
→ Microphone volume too low
→ Increase system volume to 100%
→ Enable microphone boost

Issue: "Microphone access denied"
→ Click 🔒 in address bar
→ Allow microphone
→ Refresh page

Issue: "No microphone found"
→ Plug in microphone
→ Check device manager
→ Try different USB port

Issue: Level bar doesn't move
→ Wrong microphone selected
→ Microphone is muted
→ Driver issue


💡 PRO TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Use a headset microphone (better quality)
2. Set system volume to 100%
3. Enable microphone boost (+20dB)
4. Speak 6-12 inches from microphone
5. Use in quiet environment
6. Test before using voice commands


✅ VERIFICATION STEPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. Voice recorder picks up your words
3. Words appear in query box in real-time
4. System responds to your questions

If all ✅, microphone is working correctly!
