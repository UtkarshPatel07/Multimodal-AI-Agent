━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Open Browser → http://localhost:3000

2. Upload Image
   - Drag & drop any image
   - Or click "Upload Image"

3. Ask Question (Choose One):
   
   Option A - Voice:
   ├─ Click "🎤 Press to Speak"
   ├─ Allow microphone access
   ├─ Speak your question
   └─ Click "Stop Recording"
   
   Option B - Text:
   ├─ Type question in text box
   └─ Press Enter or click "Ask"

4. Get AI Response
   ├─ Wait 5-10 seconds
   ├─ Read detailed explanation
   ├─ See visual annotations
   └─ Listen to audio response

5. Ask Follow-ups
   └─ Continue conversation naturally
