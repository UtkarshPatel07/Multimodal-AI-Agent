🎯 HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Voice Input:
1. Upload an image
2. Click "🎤 Press to Speak"
3. Allow microphone access (first time only)
4. Speak: "What is in this image?"
5. Wait for transcription
6. Get Claude's explanation!

Voice Output:
1. After getting an explanation
2. Click "🔊 Read Aloud"
3. Listen to Claude's explanation
4. Click "⏸️ Stop Speaking" to stop
